Beam: RC at L/D 12–15 continuous or 6–10 cantilever, PT at 20–25 or 10–14, rounded to 25 mm, 400 mm minimum for both. IS 456 Cl 23.2.1 alone would allow shallower beams, but they need heavy steel and rarely build economically. One-way slab: RC at L/d 30–35 continuous or 7–10 cantilever, PT at 40–45 or 10–14, plus 35 mm to the top of the bar, rounded up to 5 mm. Two-way slab: ACI 318-83 coefficient for the panel type, interpolated on ly/lx, times the clear short span; and D = ly(0.8 + 144.6fy/200 000)/(36 + 9β). Both rounded up to 5 mm; deflection only. Load-based slab thickness: the thinnest slab, in 5 mm steps from 100 mm, that passes IS 456 bending (Mu,lim), shear (k·τc, Cl 40.2.1.1) and computed deflection (Annex C): short-term with Ieff, creep with θ = 1.6, shrinkage with εcs = 0.0003; total ≤ span/250 and after finishes ≤ span/350 or 20 mm. Moments: Table 12 end span for one-way, Table 26 short span for two-way. Self weight at 25 kN/m³, steel taken as just what strength needs, sustained live load 25% (50% above 3 kN/m²), as IS 1893. PT ratios are practice, not code: IS 1343 requires deflection to be computed. First-sketch sizing, not a design.
